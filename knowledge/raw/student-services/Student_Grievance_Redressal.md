# University Guidelines on Student Grievance Redressal

## Institutional Grievance Mechanism
Under University Grants Commission (Redressal of Grievances of Students) Regulations and University Student Welfare Guidelines:

1. **Collegiate Student Grievance Redressal Committee (CSGRC)**:
   - Every institution constitutes a CSGRC comprising the Principal/Dean, senior faculty members, and a representative student member.
   - Handles student complaints regarding admission, evaluation, harassment, discrimination, or service deficiencies.

2. **How to Submit a Grievance**:
   - **Online Portal**: Students can submit an official grievance application online through the university ERP / student portal (Chalkpad) under the Student Grievance Redressal section.
   - **Written Application**: A written application addressed to the Chairperson of the CSGRC or the Office of Student Affairs / Dean of Student Welfare.
   - **Emergency / Urgent Support**: Contact the Student Affairs Office or designated student welfare helpline immediately.

3. **Timeline and Resolution**:
   - The CSGRC shall consider the student's submission and communicate its recommendation/decision within **15 days** of receiving the grievance application.

4. **Appeal to the Ombudsperson**:
   - If an aggrieved student is not satisfied with the decision of the CSGRC, they may prefer an appeal to the **Ombudsperson** appointed by the university within **15 days** from the date of receipt of the committee's decision.
   - The Ombudsperson hears the appeal and issues an authoritative ruling.
